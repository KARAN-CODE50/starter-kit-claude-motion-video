# The research prompt (step 1)

Give this to your research assistant (I used Fo by Wajo; any agent that can browse and write files works).
Attach `BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md` and `CLAUDE.md` from this kit. Fill in the three brackets.

---

I want to make a [LENGTH, e.g. 80-second] film about [TOPIC], for [AUDIENCE / WHERE IT WILL BE POSTED].
It will be built by Claude Opus 5.5 as code: a web page that draws every frame, filmed by a headless browser.
It must be able to run start to finish without asking me anything.

1. Research [TOPIC] properly: X, Reddit, GitHub, Substack, YouTube and any site that indexes this topic.
   For every fact keep the number, the source link and the date you read it. Quotes word for word, with who said them.
2. Build a roster of the people or projects that matter: handle, what they made, link to the original post, views or stars
   with the date, and what each one stands for in the story.
3. Write BRIEF.md in the exact 27-section shape of the attached BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md. Keep the section numbers.
   - Sections 3, 4 and 14: the research, the roster and every source.
   - Sections 5, 8 and 9: a look, a shot list timed to a beat grid, and a short narration script.
   - Section 23: answer every input yourself using these choices: [MUSIC / VOICE / CLIPS / HANDLE / ASPECT / BUDGET].
   - Section 24: the save-your-progress protocol from the template, unchanged.
4. Adapt the attached CLAUDE.md: palette, fonts, and anything specific to this film. Keep the render contract and the critique loop.
5. Flag anything you could not verify instead of guessing. Never invent a number, a quote, a handle or a date.

Send me both files when they are done.
